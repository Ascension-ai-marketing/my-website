Privacy

Last updated October 2, 2026.

This page explains what happens to the information you give us when you book a free discovery call on this website.

What we collect

When you book a call, the form asks for your name and email address, and optionally your phone number, your company or website, and a message. We also record the time you chose and when you booked.

What we use it for

To schedule your call, send you the details and the Google Meet link, and get in touch with you about the call.

Where it goes

  • Google Calendar. Your booking becomes an event on our calendar, with your details in its description and you invited as a guest. Google sends you the invitation.
  • Google Meet. The call happens on a Google Meet link attached to that event.
  • Google Sheets. Your details and the call time are added as a row to our private list of bookings.
  • Email. Your confirmation, and our notification of your booking, are sent from zack@ascension-marketing.ca through Proton Mail.
  • Hosting. This website runs on Vercel. Its server logs record whether a booking went through, without your name, email, phone, company or message.

Visit statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits to this website, such as which pages are viewed and which site you came from. It does not use cookies.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information you gave us, email zack@ascension-marketing.ca.
